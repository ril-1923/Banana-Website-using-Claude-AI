HEALTH PROTOCOLS

// scientifically validated
01
ENERGY OPTIMISATION

Three natural sugars — sucrose, fructose, and glucose — combined with fibre give sustained, crash-free energy. Athletes rely on bananas for pre- and mid-competition fuel.

02
GUT MICROBIOME SUPPORT

Unripe bananas are rich in resistant starch, acting as a prebiotic that feeds beneficial gut bacteria, improving digestive health and immune function.

03
CARDIAC PROTECTION

High potassium paired with low sodium helps maintain healthy blood pressure. Regular consumption is associated with a reduced risk of cardiovascular disease.

04
MOOD REGULATION

Bananas contain tryptophan, which the body converts to serotonin. Combined with Vitamin B6, they naturally support mood stability and cognitive function.

05
ANTIOXIDANT DEFENCE

Rich in dopamine and catechins — powerful antioxidants linked to reduced risk of heart disease and degenerative diseases. Their concentration increases as bananas ripen.
